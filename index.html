Small screens, big firms.

Dolus Games makes pixel-art management games in the spirit of the Japanese handheld classics: one office on screen, a decision every minute, and a career that can end badly.

Lord of Wall Street

Brooklyn, January 1985. You are Lou Street, and your trading firm is a garage with a desk in it. Hire analysts, launch funds, win clients, and survive everything the market throws at you between now and the year 2100.

Free until Black Monday, October 1987 — more than an hour of play. One purchase then unlocks the rest of the career. No ads, no account, works offline.

More about the game

House rules

Free, then one price

Every game starts free. If you like it, a single purchase unlocks everything. Nothing else is ever sold.

Your save stays with you

No ads, no account, no tracking. The game runs on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

Real words, explained once

Funds, vehicles, high yield, drawdowns: the real vocabulary, each term explained in one plain sentence the first time it matters.

Losing is allowed

A tycoon game without a game over is a screensaver. Firms go under; the rare trophies are rare on purpose.

Who we are

Dolus Games is a one-person studio founded in 2026 and published by Dolus LLC. We build small, dense simulation games for phones and tablets, in French and English first, and we take balancing more seriously than graphics — which is saying something, because we take the graphics seriously too.
